yield: 6 to 8 servings (about 3 1/2 quarts)
preparation_time: 20 minutes
cooking_time: 6 to 14 hours

ingredients:
- 1 large Crock-Pot
- 3 Slices bacon, diced
- 1/2 Ib. hot Italian link sausage, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1/2 lb. ground chuck
- 2 medium onions, chopped
- 1 small green pepper, seeded & chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 jalapeno pepper, seeded & chopped
- 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 to 3 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp dry mustard
- 1/4 tsp freshly ground pepper
- 2 cans (14-16 0z each) Italian-style tomatoes
- 1 can (16 oz.) pinto beans, drained
- 1 can (16 oz.) kidney beans, drained


directions:
- In a large skillet, brown bacon pieces until crisp. Remove from skillet; drain well.
-Brown sausage and ground chuck with onions over medium heat; drain and add to Crock-Pot.
- Add bacon and remaining ingredients; stir well.
- Cover and cook on Low setting for 6 to 14 hours.
- Taste for seasoning.

notes:
- Use Hot Mexican Chili Powder for part of chili powder to increase heat.
